# Launchpad competitive product research

Research date: 2026-09-30. Scope: public, unauthenticated product surfaces and local source-of-truth documents. No wallet connected, form submitted, transaction created, service started, production accessed or implementation changed.

## Executive summary

TEKKTEAM needs a connected product lifecycle, not another launch button. The strongest recurring pattern is a launched coin attached to an identifiable operator, a persistent destination, observable activity and a reason to return. Trading is one possible utility; it is not the definition of a launchpad.

Airpad packages a coin with an ongoing AI-host experience. Trench Dots connects independently useful calls and trading squads with a newer coin-launch journey. Bagwork makes coin + Agent + Wallet the creation unit and links it to public operation, performance, fees and an Agent marketplace. These are different systems, not interchangeable templates.

Recommend a launchpad-first TEKKTEAM with independent Agent identity, optional token configuration, explicit owner-approved launch, verified public discovery and a persistent Agent/token destination. After launch, offer only capabilities that are actually available. A launched token must not silently authorize trading.

### Evidence discipline

**OBSERVED** means visible public UI, navigation or disclosure. **CLAIMED** means the product says it performs a backend/on-chain action; that action was not tested. **INFERENCE** means interpretation. **TARGET** means recommendation, not shipped TEKKTEAM behavior.

All meaningful navigation destinations listed below were opened in the browser, including the Bagwork SPA routes. Public example details were inspected: Trench squad 201 and Bagwork Agent 178. No ownership-gated journey was executed. Web text extraction failed for Airpad/Trench and returned an empty Bagwork shell; the rendered browser supplied the evidence. Market numbers are transient snapshots, not independently verified chain/accounting certifications.

## What a launchpad actually is

A launchpad is a product that coordinates asset creation and launch with discoverability, trustworthy lifecycle status and a durable post-launch destination. In an Agent launchpad, an associated Agent provides an explicitly defined ongoing capability and observable relationship to that asset. This is a product definition derived from these examples, not a regulatory classification.

| Product type | Primary job | What it does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Token creator | Produce metadata/mint/creation transaction | Discovery, community or ongoing utility |
| Launchpad | Coordinate creation, release, discovery and launch lifecycle | Autonomous trading or guaranteed liquidity/success |
| Trading bot platform | Configure funded strategy execution and track results | Token issuance or a launch ecosystem |
| Agent platform | Configure identities/capabilities and observe operation | A launched asset |
| Agent launchpad | Connect a launch to an Agent, persistent utility and public lifecycle | Permission to trade merely because launch succeeded |

A button does not change TEKKTEAM's product center if creation remains hidden, Home still leads with a trading workforce and a confirmed launch has no discovery/return destination.

## Airpad product map

Sources: [Home](https://www.airpad.club/), [Launch](https://www.airpad.club/#launch), [How](https://www.airpad.club/#how), [Studio](https://www.airpad.club/#studio), [Token economics](https://www.airpad.club/#token), [FAQ](https://www.airpad.club/#faq).

**OBSERVED:** One scrolling site, with navigation to those sections rather than separate application pages. Home leads with coin launch and ongoing host utility; launch is the primary CTA, listening is the secondary demonstration. Configuration combines name/ticker, host personality, voice, schedule and creator-fee allocation with a live review preview. The fee control defaults to 20%; FAQ describes a 5–50% host budget. A separate 5% creator-fee purchase loop for the platform coin is described.

**CLAIMED:** The host runs an X Space, discusses market activity, welcomes holders, answers questions and manages speakers. Creator fees fund the service; FAQ says reduced fees shorten shows. The studio is explicitly a demonstration. Its changing listener count/transcript is not proof of a live launched customer room.

**Wallet boundary:** Connect and a signing CTA are visible. The form also describes signing as free/non-transactional while other copy describes launching with one signature. The complete creation transaction, payer, costs, approvals and host provisioning could not be verified without connecting/submitting. Do not infer a safe launch contract from marketing compression.

**INFERENCE core loop:** Configure coin + utility → launch claim → recurring room/activity → audience/trading → fee-supported utility → continued participation. The portable principle is a useful ongoing destination, NOT adopting voice or X Spaces.

No public launched-coin directory, per-coin account dashboard, wallet management, leaderboard or durable launch failure states was observed in the available navigation. This is an observation limit, not proof those systems do not exist elsewhere.

## Trench Dots product map

Sources: [Home](https://trenchdots.fun/), [Calls](https://trenchdots.fun/calls), [Hire](https://trenchdots.fun/hire), [Launch](https://trenchdots.fun/launch), [Squads](https://trenchdots.fun/squads), [Coins](https://trenchdots.fun/coins), [Burns](https://trenchdots.fun/burns), [How](https://trenchdots.fun/how), [Public launched squad](https://trenchdots.fun/squads/201).

### Two entrances, one persistent squad

The homepage still presents squad trading of pump.fun calls as a core promise, alongside a prominent newer launchpad section. It is not evidence that every competitor began launchpad-first. Visitors can hire a trading squad without creating a coin, or launch a coin with a squad. Both resolve to an identifiable squad and wallet.

Those transaction steps are **CLAIMED**, not executed in this audit. Residual launch reserve is described as squad capital. Fee allocation is 90% retained as capital, 10% platform-token buy/burn, separately reported from trading PnL.

### Calls

A call is a timestamped market-discovery event, not a trade or launch receipt. Categories observed: FOMO thesis, community, boosted, new profiles and trending. Origins include DEX Screener paid boosts/new profiles, GeckoTerminal trending, external theses and community wallets. Rows show token, origin/time, market cap at call/current/peak, current and peak multiples and squad participation counts. Not every row identifies individual squads directly.

The scoring disclosure defines a hit at 2x the market cap at call and caller ranking by average peak. That measures an observed price path, not an achievable fill, realized PnL or guaranteed return. Paid promotion is a source, not an endorsement. The board remains useful to observers with no launched coin or funded squad.

### Hire and squads

Hire exposes finder → buyer → seller jobs, with exact policy consequences beneath understandable choices. Ownership-message-only hiring is described separately from later funding. The directory ranks by ROI/PnL/portfolio/trades/newest, filters operating state, and shows win rate/last trade. HOUSE squads are explicitly labeled Paper despite using real prices.

Squad 201 connects wallet/owner, state/controls, launched coin/CA/creation transaction, fees, policy, equity, decisions, positions, trades and money flows. It shows failed entries as well as completed actions. The recorded launch sequence includes awaiting funds → creating coin → live coin; this is stronger lifecycle evidence than a homepage promise, but not independent RPC verification.

**Important boundary:** The How disclosure says squads do not buy platform-launched coins or the platform coin for ordinary trading; a coin's optional dev buy is the exception. An Agent being linked to a token does NOT imply automatic support buying. Burns are a separately described economic operation.

## Bagwork product map

Sources: [Home](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/), [Launch](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/launch), [Agents](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/agents), [Tokens](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/tokens), [Market](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/market), [Skins](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/skins), [Agent Burns](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/burns), [Dev Burns](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/dev-burns), [How](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/how), [Public Agent](https://bagworkagent.fun/#/agent/178).

Home joins coin launch to an autonomous Agent and its separate wallet. It places launch CTA beside character roles, new coin/Agent arrivals, rankings, public trading activity and active Agents. This makes the subsequent operation visible before creation.

Launch configures character/coin artwork, metadata/social link, Agent identity, starting capital and a strategy preset/custom option. The preview explains size, exits, hold time and capital. Default review shows 0.5 SOL starting capital + 0.05 SOL launch reserve = 0.55 SOL. Residual reserve is described as remaining capital. No separate optional dev-buy control was observed. Wallet approval, server-created Agent Wallet, wallet-created coin and automatic operation are **CLAIMED**; no submission was made.

The Agent page provides the durable bridge: Agent identity/level/status → associated coin with mint and creation link → wallet/owner → decisions and failed entries → open positions and trade links → money flows → fee claims → strategy. Coin website is configured to point back to that page. The page states that platform-created coins are excluded from ordinary Agent trading.

Tokens is an external tradable-token board, NOT simply an inventory of Bagwork-created coins. It shows price, market cap, liquidity, volume, momentum, age, holding counts and eligibility. Some threshold copy rendered unavailable/undefined: do not import that UX defect.

Market is an **Agent ownership marketplace**, not a token-price page. Listings combine price, coin, track record and fee history. Disclosures describe sale restrictions, payment, ownership transfer, seller payout, refunds and funding the empty acquired wallet. These are claims, not audited settlement/custody guarantees. TEKKTEAM should not adopt this feature merely to fill its Market route.

Skins adds identity collection/NFT ownership. How describes profit-linked career progression and creator rewards. Burns pages provide distinct fee/buy/burn views. There is a material copy inconsistency: Home/Launch says all fees stay with the Agent, while Burns and the inspected detail specify 10% burn / 90% Agent. Economics require explicit authoritative policy; do not resolve this contradiction by guessing. Dev Burns exposed a raw simulation error and described automatic retry—an anti-pattern for TEKKTEAM's safe user-facing diagnostics and uncertain-outcome policy.

No standalone Calls route was observed. Market inputs, decisions and public trades provide discovery/activity without pretending to be community calls. Shill controls were observed but not activated; no external post was created.

## Launchpad loop

**INFERENCE from observed systems:**

```text
Discover existing units/activity
  → configure identity + optional asset + useful capability
  → review ownership, permissions and itemized costs
  → separately approve required funding/launch actions
  → reconcile verified launch
  → persistent public Agent/token destination
  → operate only authorized capabilities
  → observe activity, market and separately accounted economics
  → follow/share/compare/discover again
```

Funding is conditional, not a universal Agent-creation requirement. Utility may be non-trading. Failed/pending/unknown launch branches must remain visible and must not continue into an invented live state. Discovery is an entry point and a return loop, not a guarantee of demand.

## Calls / signals analysis

**Recommendation:** yes to a future provenance-backed Agent Signals concept; no to immediately adding a primary Calls page with no genuine event pipeline. It solves “what did an Agent notice, when, why, and what happened afterward?” independently of ownership or launch. Start as a read-only activity/discovery projection if authentic data exists.

Required future contract: event ID, source/type, Agent association where real, token mint, observed time/slot, decision/reason, market baseline, current/peak with methodology and missing/stale status. A signal is not a trade; a rejected signal is not a failed buy. Public sharing needs privacy/moderation review. Peak multiple must never be labeled realized PnL. Community-submitted calls are a separate abuse/scoring scope, not a default MVP dependency.

## Agent configuration analysis

Use understandable jobs first: what the Agent observes, what actions it may propose, how capital is bounded, and when it exits/pauses. Then disclose precise rules and costs. Trench's independent role choices and Bagwork's strategy presets show two valid models; TEKKTEAM need not invent three separate Agents to borrow the comprehension principle.

Keep identity/character, strategy, Risk, mode, Wallet, launch configuration and authorization distinct. Presets may simplify presentation but cannot obscure actual risk overrides, minimum trade size, fees or stop-loss fill uncertainty. Never translate “configure” into “fund and start Mainnet automatically.” Preserve owner approval and current safety gates.

## Post-launch retention loop

Return for verified launch progress, actual Agent activity/reasons, token-market changes, capability status, wallet/capital needs and separately accounted results. Public visitors return to discover and compare units; creators return to operate and inspect their own unit.

Token price, creator revenue, trading PnL, deposits, launch costs and platform allocations are different measures. A fee inflow is not strategy alpha. Linking coin to Agent does not create token-holder rights to the wallet/profits. Define economic rights explicitly before presenting them.

An idle/paused Agent must still have a useful truthful page: coin status, public metadata, activity history and next safe action. Do not manufacture trading to make retention look active.

### What TEKKTEAM already has

Stable Agent IDs; Schema V2 identity-only reads; separate encrypted Agent Wallet custody; owner authentication/ownership; existing Pump prepare/approval/status/attempt diagnostics; token draft/receipt presentation; Wallet workspace; Paper decisions/history/performance; bounded Real infrastructure and network/reconciliation safeguards; authored robot/office identity and shared Home components.

### Recommended destination responsibilities

- **Home:** lead with Agent launch promise + launch CTA + verified recent units/activity; supporting Agent capability proof. Do not give coins, signals, market and trading equal hero weight.
- **Launchpad:** creation entry, launch discovery and lifecycle resume/status. Include itemized review and the existing authoritative approval path. No duplicate transaction flow.
- **Agents:** persistent identities/operators, capability state, owner controls and safe public profile projections. Token optional.
- **Tokens:** configured/launched asset inventory, verified mint/receipt/market relationship; drafts clearly distinct.
- **Market:** broader read-only market discovery with provenance/freshness. Not an Agent ownership marketplace unless separately scoped and authorized.
- **Signals:** prospective timestamped observations; subordinate until actual data/reason-to-return validates a dedicated destination.

## Design research: principles, not pixels

Desktop surfaces inspected visually: Airpad launch form/preview, public Bagwork pages, Trench launch/detail. At 390px, Trench and Bagwork launch screenshots showed single-column workflow and horizontally scrollable navigation. Trench retains a bottom review shortcut, keeping total cost accessible through its long form. Bagwork places character selection early, making the preview tangible but pushing configuration/costs farther down. Airpad mobile behavior was not reliably verified; its screenshot remained desktop width, so no mobile claim is made for it.

Above fold, communicate the object and next action before data density. Keep a resumable review accessible during long setup; its purpose is cost/permission comprehension, not merely conversion. Use compact tables/rows for repeated market and activity data; reserve rich art for identity. Display funded/live/paused/failed as separate states. Empty fees/burns/positions should state “none yet” with the reason, not fabricate numbers. Preserve historical failures; don't rank failed/no-history entries as performance leaders merely because zero exceeds a loss. On mobile, prioritize consequence/cost/CTA and progressive disclosure rather than shrinking desktop columns.

## TEKKTEAM opportunities and anti-patterns

Prioritize: lifecycle-connected launch destination → truthful public discovery → persistent Agent/token unit → capability and outcome activity → separately defined economics. Calls, NFT cosmetics, transferable Agents, burns, rewards and voice are NOT automatic requirements.

Avoid automatic launch-to-trading permission, concealed capital requirements, confusing creator wallet with Agent payer, promoting prepared mint to launched coin, public leakage of private owner state, mixing Paper/Real rank, claiming fee inflows as PnL, peak-only signal marketing, token support-buy promises, raw errors, unbounded retries and marketing copy contradicting backend economics. Do not copy characters, roles/names, logos, artwork or form copy.

## Questions still unresolved

1. What useful launch-linked Agent capability is genuinely available at MVP with Live closed?
2. Is public discovery opt-in; which owner/activity fields are public-safe?
3. Who is the Mainnet on-chain creator/payer under the existing launch semantics, and who receives/controls fees? Competitive reference semantics do not override the current owner-payer contract.
4. What rights, if any, does a token convey? Do not imply wallet ownership/profit rights.
5. What creator-fee handling exists or is proposed, and how are costs, revenue, PnL and allocations reconciled?
6. What verified market feed supports launched-coin discovery before external indexing?
7. Are signals authentic durable observations or only saved scan rows; what scoring/moderation is appropriate?
8. What is the resume/recovery experience across prepared/expired/unknown/confirmed launch states?
9. Are a tokenless Agent journey and coin+Agent launch journey both MVP entrances?
10. How is post-launch utility funded without forcing unsafe autonomous activation?
